Cookie Policy

Last Updated: April 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Boostrava Ads Network ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") uses cookies, pixels, web beacons, SDKs, and similar tracking technologies (collectively "Cookies") on our website www.boostrava.com and within our advertising platform (DSP/SSP). This Cookie Policy explains:

  • What Cookies are and how we use them.
  • The types of Cookies we deploy.
  • Your choices and how to control Cookies, including opting out of personalised advertising.
  • Our compliance with the IAB Europe Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF) v2.3 and Google Consent Mode v2.

By continuing to use our website or platform, you consent to our use of Cookies as described in this policy, unless you have set your browser or device to refuse Cookies.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, provide information to website owners, and enable personalised content and advertising. Other tracking technologies (pixels, SDKs) serve similar purposes in apps and emails.

  • First-party Cookies – set by Boostrava directly.
  • Third-party Cookies – set by our partners (e.g., ad exchanges, analytics providers).

3. Why We Use Cookies

We use Cookies for the following purposes:

PurposeDescription
Strictly NecessaryEssential for the website to function (e.g., logging into your account, security, load balancing). Cannot be disabled.
FunctionalityRemember your preferences (language, region, dashboard layout).
Performance / AnalyticsCollect anonymous information about how visitors use our site (pages visited, time on site, errors). We use Google Analytics and other tools to improve our services.
Targeting / AdvertisingDeliver relevant ads to you on our platform and across the web. Measure ad performance, limit the number of times you see an ad, and build audience profiles. These Cookies are placed by us and by our advertising partners (demand-side platforms, supply-side platforms, ad exchanges).

5. Cookies and Your Consent (IAB TCF v2.3)

Boostrava participates in the IAB Europe Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF) v2.3 and complies with its specifications. This means:

  • When you visit our website or use a publisher’s app where we serve ads, we (or our consent management platform) will request your consent for:
    • Storing and/or accessing information on your device (Cookie consent).
    • Processing your personal data for personalised advertising, ad measurement, audience research, and other purposes.
  • Your consent choices are stored in a special Cookie (__eoi) and communicated to all participating ad partners via the TCF global vendor list.

Google Consent Mode v2 is also implemented on our website, meaning that our tags adjust their behaviour based on your consent choices (e.g., Google Ads tags only fire if you have consented to analytics and advertising Cookies).

6. How to Control and Delete Cookies

6.1 Browser Controls

Most web browsers allow you to:

  • See what Cookies are stored and delete them (individually or all).
  • Block third-party Cookies.
  • Block all Cookies from specific websites.
  • Enable a “Do Not Track” (DNT) signal (note: DNT is not universally respected; we honour the Global Privacy Control instead).

Instructions for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

6.2 Mobile Device Controls

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Advertising → Limit Ad Tracking (or “Ask App Not to Track”).
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation.

6.3 Global Privacy Control (GPC)

We recognise the Global Privacy Control signal as a valid opt-out request under the CCPA/CPRA. If your browser or extension sends a GPC signal, we will treat it as an opt-out of the sale/sharing of your personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can download GPC at globalprivacycontrol.org.

6.4 Industry Opt-Out Pages

You can opt out of interest-based advertising from multiple companies at once:

  • YourOnlineChoices (EU): www.youronlinechoices.com
  • NAI Opt-Out (US): optout.networkadvertising.org
  • DAA Opt-Out (US): optout.aboutads.info
  • DAAC (Canada): youradchoices.ca

6.5 Our Dedicated Opt-Out Page

We provide a simple opt-out page at: www.boostrava.com/opt-out — after confirming your choice, we will set a persistent opt-out Cookie that tells our system not to target you with personalised ads.

7. Cookies and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA)

Under California law, the use of targeting/advertising Cookies may constitute a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. We provide a visible notice at the point of collection (Cookie banner) and a clear mechanism to opt out. As required by the CPPA final regulations effective January 1, 2026, we also offer a confirmation page where you can verify that your opt-out signal (including GPC) has been processed.

To request confirmation, please email privacy@boostrava.com with subject “CCPA Opt-Out Confirmation”.

8. Cookies and the EU ePrivacy Directive / GDPR

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland:

  • Strictly necessary Cookies are placed without consent.
  • All other Cookies (functionality, analytics, targeting) are placed only after you have given explicit, informed consent via our Cookie banner.
  • You have the right to withdraw consent at any time by clicking the “Cookie Settings” link in our website footer.

9. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time due to changes in technology, legal requirements, or our business practices. Any material changes will be notified by posting a prominent notice on our website or via email. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy indicates when it was last revised.
