Advertising Privacy Choices

This page explains available choices related to cookies and advertising technologies used on Boost RAVA properties. The exact controls available to you can depend on your browser, device, region and the services involved.

Browser and cookie controls

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect website functionality. Where a consent or preference control is presented, use that interface to update your choices.

Industry opt-out resources

Our Cookie Policy lists recognised industry resources that may provide additional advertising choices in supported regions. Those services are operated independently and their availability may vary.

Contact us

If you need help understanding Boost RAVA privacy choices, contact us with the relevant website, device and request details. Do not send passwords or sensitive authentication information.

Contact Boost RAVA

What this page can and cannot do

Boost RAVA can explain the privacy and advertising choices available on its own properties. Some advertising choices are controlled by your browser, device, publisher, consent platform or another service provider, so a single request may not disable every form of advertising or measurement across the internet.

Before you contact us

Please include the relevant Boost RAVA property or page, your country or region, device type, browser and a short description of the choice you are trying to exercise. Do not include passwords, payment credentials or other sensitive authentication information.

Effect of blocking technologies

Blocking or deleting cookies and similar storage may reset preferences, sign you out of services or reduce functionality. Privacy choices can also differ by browser profile and device, so you may need to repeat them in more than one environment.

Helpful answers

Questions people ask about this topic

Concise answers based on Boost RAVA's stated services and availability rules.

What are advertising privacy choices?

Advertising privacy choices are controls or requests that can affect how supported advertising technologies use cookies, identifiers or other signals, subject to the implementation and applicable law.

Will every advertising signal disappear after a privacy choice?

Not necessarily. Some data may still be processed for security, fraud prevention, measurement, legal obligations or strictly necessary service functions where permitted.
