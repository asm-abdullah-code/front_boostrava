DOOH, EXPLAINED

Outdoor Advertising Meets Digital Technology.

DOOH means digital out-of-home advertising: digital displays used for advertising in public, commercial or destination environments. It can include digital billboards, LED displays, mall screens, retail displays, transit displays, building screens and other eligible digital inventory.

TRADITIONAL OOH

Fixed creative, physical placement

Classic billboards and static placements rely on physical production and fixed media cycles.

DOOH

Digital screen, flexible schedule

Digital screens can support scheduled creative changes, different booking windows, playback reporting and digital workflow controls where the screen system supports them.

WHY DOOH

Be Seen Beyond the Browser.

DOOH turns physical screen presence into bookable digital media without pretending every screen has the same technology, measurement or buying model.

↗

High visibility

Large public or commercial screens can create a prominent real-world brand presence.

↗

Location-based planning

Choose from eligible countries, cities, areas, locations, screen networks or individual screens.

↗

Time as inventory

Hourly, daily where applicable, weekly, monthly and custom windows can become bookable media units.

↗

Digital creative

Use approved static, video or other supported digital creative based on the screen technology.

↗

Flexible booking

Match booking duration to a launch, event, promotion or longer brand campaign.

↗

Measured where data exists

Use playback, booking, partner or estimated audience data without turning estimates into guaranteed impressions.

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU BOOK

DOOH Advertising Questions, Answered Clearly.

These answers are written for people first and also provide concise factual definitions for search and answer engines.

What is DOOH advertising?

DOOH means digital out-of-home advertising. It uses digital billboards, LED displays and other eligible public or commercial screens to deliver advertising outside the browser and app environment.

What is a digital billboard?

A digital billboard is an electronic display used for outdoor or public-space advertising. Screen specifications, playback rules, operating hours and availability vary by media owner or screen partner.

How does DOOH advertising work?

An advertiser selects eligible inventory, location, timing and booking duration, supplies an approved creative, confirms the booking and receives delivery or playback reporting where the inventory supports it.

Can I book a DOOH screen by the hour?

Hourly booking can be offered when the screen owner or inventory partner makes hourly slots available. It is not guaranteed for every screen.

Can I book a screen weekly?

Yes where weekly inventory is available. The final booking depends on the selected screen, dates, operating schedule and publisher approval.

Can I book a screen monthly?

Monthly bookings can be supported where the screen owner offers that pricing and availability model.

Can advertisers choose specific locations?

Yes. DOOH planning is location-led, so advertisers can select from available countries, cities, areas, locations, screen networks or individual screens depending on the inventory.

Can Bangladesh businesses advertise on DOOH?

Yes. Bangladesh-based advertisers can explore available DOOH inventory in Bangladesh and, where partner inventory exists, selected international markets.

Can I advertise across multiple cities?

Yes as a campaign-planning concept, provided eligible screens are available in each selected city. Availability is confirmed before booking.

Can screen owners join Boost RAVA?

Eligible screen owners can apply to list DOOH inventory. Screen ownership or authorization, location, technical details, availability and content-policy requirements should be verified before a screen is treated as live inventory.

How do I list my LED screen?

A screen-owner workflow can include registration, screen submission, location verification, technical details, availability, pricing, review and activation. The exact production workflow depends on the DOOH publisher module status.

How is DOOH pricing calculated?

Pricing can be configured by screen and booking model such as hourly, daily where applicable, weekly, monthly or custom. Peak, event or seasonal pricing should only be treated as live when implemented.

Can I set my own screen pricing?

A screen owner may be able to configure rates where that publisher control is enabled. Final commercial rules can also depend on platform policies and approval settings.

Can I manage screen availability?

Availability management is part of the intended screen-owner workflow. Live status depends on the production implementation of the DOOH inventory module.

Can I upload multiple creatives?

Multiple creatives and creative rotation can be supported where the selected screen, playback system and campaign workflow allow them.

Can DOOH be programmatic?

Yes, some DOOH inventory can be bought through programmatic workflows. Boost RAVA also supports the concept of direct time-based booking, so not every DOOH placement should be described as auction-based.

Does Boost RAVA own the screens?

No ownership claim is made for third-party or publisher-provided screens. Eligible inventory is positioned as Publisher Network, Screen Partner or DOOH Inventory Partner supply.

Is every screen available in every country?

No. Screen availability depends on publisher or partner supply, location, schedule, technical compatibility and campaign requirements.

How is DOOH reach measured?

DOOH reporting can use playback data, estimated impressions, footfall or partner-provided audience estimates where available. Estimated audience metrics must remain clearly distinct from directly measured events.

Are DOOH impressions guaranteed?

No. Inventory, measurement, pricing, reach and impression estimates can vary by screen owner, partner and data source. Any forecast or calculator output on this page is an estimate, not guaranteed delivery.
