ADVERTISER GUIDE

Frequently Asked Questions About Advertising Online

Key answers regarding digital advertising in Bangladesh, campaign budgets, and programmatic buying.

How do I advertise online in Bangladesh with Boost RAVA?+

Create a free advertiser account on Boost RAVA, configure your campaign objective, choose your target audience (by Bangladesh location, device, or topic), upload your display or HTML5 creative, set your budget, and launch. Your campaign starts delivering across top publisher sites within minutes.

What is the minimum budget required to start digital advertising in Bangladesh?+

Boost RAVA is an accessible online advertising platform for both high-growth startups and established brands. You can start with flexible daily or lifetime budgets, retaining complete control over spend with no hidden fees.

What ad formats are supported on the Boost RAVA advertiser platform?+

Boost RAVA supports standard display banners (300×250, 728×90, 320×50, 970×250, 300×600), interactive HTML5 ads, high-impact rich media, in-stream and out-stream video ads, and connected screen placements.

How does conversion tracking work for programmatic campaigns?+

Boost RAVA includes built-in Server-to-Server (S2S) postback conversion tracking and UTM tracking macro helpers. You can track leads, purchases, app downloads, and custom conversion events with full attribution.

How does targeted advertising in Bangladesh work on Boost RAVA?+

Advertisers can target consumers by division, district, carrier network, smartphone brand, operating system, and content category across leading Bengali and English publisher networks.

Can I calculate estimated campaign reach and impressions before launching?+

Yes. Boost RAVA provides a media planning calculator that estimates your expected impressions, clicks, and effective CPA based on your budget, target CPM, and format choices.
