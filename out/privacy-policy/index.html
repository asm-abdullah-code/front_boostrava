LEGAL · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

How Boost RAVA handles information across its website, advertiser and publisher services, programmatic advertising workflows, reporting, creative tools and related technology.

Effective: 30 August 2026 · Last updated: 30 August 2026

Boost RAVA LegalPrivacy PolicyTerms & ConditionsPayment PolicyCookie PolicyContact
This policy is written for the Boost RAVA advertising ecosystem. It explains categories of data and purposes without claiming that every optional integration, identifier or advanced advertising capability is enabled for every customer, market or campaign.
01

1. Scope and who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to Boost RAVA websites, account and dashboard experiences, advertiser campaign workflows, publisher monetization workflows, creative and reporting tools, programmatic advertising technology, support interactions and other services that link to this policy.

It covers information relating to advertisers, agencies, publishers, website or inventory owners, business contacts, platform users and visitors. Advertising delivery may also involve device, browser, network, contextual, event or similar technical information associated with an ad request or interaction.

  • Website visitors and prospective customers
  • Advertiser, agency and campaign users
  • Publishers, property owners and monetization users
  • Business, partnership and support contacts
  • Technical events generated when ads, pages, tags or platform functions are requested or used
02

2. Information we may collect

Account and business information

  • Name, business name, email address, phone number and account identifiers
  • Role, organization, country, preferred currency or account settings where supplied
  • Publisher property, domain, website, inventory and verification information
  • Advertiser campaign, creative, destination URL, budget, schedule and targeting configuration

Billing, transaction and payout information

We may process transaction references, account balances, billing records, payment status, invoices, payout details and related operational records needed to fund campaigns, reconcile transactions, process eligible publisher payments, prevent abuse and maintain financial records. Payment methods available to a user can vary by account, country and platform configuration. This policy does not represent that any named third-party payment provider is universally available.

Advertising and technical information

  • IP address and approximate location derived from technical signals where permitted
  • Browser, device type, operating system, language, network and request metadata
  • Ad request, impression, click, conversion or postback events where configured
  • Publisher property, ad unit, placement, creative, campaign and delivery identifiers
  • Timestamp, frequency, diagnostic, security, invalid-traffic and quality signals
  • Contextual information about a page or placement where contextual processing is used

Support and communications

If you contact us, we may retain the message, contact details, attachments and related correspondence necessary to respond, investigate, document or resolve the request.

03

3. How we use information

  • Create, authenticate, secure and administer accounts
  • Build, configure, deliver, measure and report advertising campaigns
  • Operate publisher properties, ad units, inventory, earnings and payout workflows
  • Perform ad serving, campaign decisioning, frequency, scheduling and applicable targeting
  • Provide analytics, attribution or conversion reporting where configured
  • Detect suspicious activity, invalid traffic, fraud, abuse and policy violations
  • Maintain service reliability, troubleshoot errors and improve platform performance
  • Provide support, operational notices, security messages and requested communications
  • Meet accounting, legal, compliance, audit and recordkeeping obligations
04

4. Programmatic advertising and automated processing

Programmatic advertising uses software to evaluate eligible advertising opportunities and apply campaign rules, inventory availability, targeting settings, pricing or auction logic, creative eligibility and delivery controls. Depending on the workflow, decisions can occur rapidly and may use technical or contextual signals.

Boost RAVA may provide AI-assisted, recommendation or automation-oriented interfaces where implemented. We do not state that every AI, forecasting, bidding or optimization capability is active in every production environment. Where a feature is presented as a planning estimate, recommendation, verification item or roadmap capability, it should not be interpreted as a guaranteed automated decision or outcome.

05

5. Cookies, tags and similar technologies

We may use cookies, local storage, pixels, tags, scripts and comparable technologies for essential site operation, security, preferences, measurement and advertising functions where applicable. The categories and choices are explained in the Cookie Policy. Browser controls or consent choices can affect whether non-essential technologies operate.

06

6. How information may be shared

We do not need to sell a person’s private account information to operate an advertising platform. Information may, however, be disclosed or transmitted when reasonably necessary to provide the service, complete a requested advertising or publisher workflow, protect the platform or comply with law.

  • Service and infrastructure providers acting for operational purposes
  • Advertising ecosystem participants when necessary for an eligible campaign, inventory or measurement workflow
  • Professional advisers, auditors or compliance providers under appropriate obligations
  • Authorities or other parties when disclosure is legally required or necessary to protect rights, safety, security or the integrity of the service
  • A successor or relevant party in a legitimate corporate restructuring, financing, acquisition or transfer, subject to applicable obligations
07

7. Data minimization, retention and security

We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including service delivery, dispute handling, security, fraud prevention, accounting and legal obligations. Retention periods can differ by data type and account status.

We use administrative, technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the service. No internet, advertising or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and users must also protect credentials, devices, API secrets and authorized account access.

08

8. International processing

Programmatic advertising can involve advertisers, publishers, infrastructure and inventory in different countries. Information may therefore be processed in jurisdictions other than the user’s own when necessary to provide eligible services. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we will use measures appropriate to the relevant processing.

09

9. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on applicable law and the relationship with Boost RAVA, a person may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or other privacy choices. Rights are not absolute and may be limited where records must be retained for security, fraud prevention, contractual, accounting or legal reasons.

Requests should identify the relevant account or interaction and the right being exercised. We may need to verify identity or authority before acting on a request. Advertising opt-out or cookie choices can also be available through browser, device or platform controls where applicable.

10

10. Children

Boost RAVA is a business advertising technology service and is not intended for children to create advertiser or publisher accounts. Users must not knowingly provide children’s personal information to the platform in violation of applicable law.

11

11. Third-party websites and integrations

Campaign destinations, publisher properties and third-party services are controlled by their respective operators. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. A link, tag, API, integration or inventory relationship does not make Boost RAVA responsible for a third party’s independent privacy practices.

12

12. Changes and contact

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, legal requirements or operational practices evolve. The effective or last-updated date on this page identifies the current published version.

Questions about these policies can be submitted through the Boost RAVA Contact page. Please include enough account, campaign, publisher-property, billing, or technical context for us to identify the matter without sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.
