AI PROGRAMMATIC FAQ

Questions advertisers, publishers and agencies ask before using AI.

Direct answers are written for people first and structured so search and answer engines can extract the same factual information without hidden claims.

What is AI programmatic advertising?

AI programmatic advertising combines automated media buying with AI-assisted analysis, forecasting or recommendations. The exact level of automation depends on the data, controls and production features available.

How does Boost RAVA use AI?

Boost RAVA positions AI as an intelligence layer around programmatic planning, analysis and optimization. Advanced functions are shown with explicit LIVE, VERIFY or ROADMAP status so marketing copy does not imply unavailable automation.

Can AI create a media plan?

The page includes a transparent planning experience that can organize budget, audience, format and schedule inputs. A production AI media planner should be treated as a roadmap capability until its backend is verified.

Can AI calculate campaign costs?

Yes. Transparent planning calculations can estimate impressions, clicks, conversions, CPC, CPM and CPA from user-provided assumptions. These outputs are estimates, not guaranteed outcomes.

Can AI forecast impressions and conversions?

Model-based forecasting can estimate ranges when sufficient historical and campaign data exist. Until a production forecasting model is verified, Boost RAVA should label these outputs as forecast or planning estimates.

Can AI optimize campaign budgets?

AI can recommend budget changes based on performance signals. Automatic budget changes should only be described as live where an approved auto-optimization system is actually enabled.

Can AI recommend bids?

AI can be used to recommend bid levels from campaign, placement and historical signals. Production AI bidding remains verification-dependent and should not be presented as guaranteed best-bid selection.

Does AI work with RTB?

AI can support RTB decisioning by evaluating eligible opportunities before a bid decision. The auction still follows the programmatic delivery architecture configured for the campaign and inventory.

Does AI support OpenRTB?

OpenRTB-specific AI decisioning should only be claimed when the relevant OpenRTB connection and runtime are verified in production.

Can AI optimize publisher revenue?

AI can analyze publisher-side signals such as eCPM, fill, format and inventory performance to generate recommendations. Automated yield optimization is roadmap or verification-dependent unless production controls are confirmed.

Can AI analyze Rich Media campaigns?

AI-assisted analysis can compare supported rich-media delivery and interaction data when those events are collected. Advanced interaction intelligence depends on the actual tracking available for each format.

Can I ask questions about my campaign data?

A natural-language “Ask Your Data” interface is a roadmap capability unless a production analytics assistant is connected to verified campaign data.

Can AI automatically change my campaign?

Only where explicit automation is enabled and permissioned. The safe default is recommendation-first: explain the suggestion, data used, assumptions and expected impact before an action is applied.

How does AI explain its recommendations?

An explainable recommendation should show what it suggests, why, which data informed it, the calculation or model basis, confidence or caveats, and the action available to the user.

Is AI forecasting guaranteed?

No. Forecasts are estimates. They should communicate assumptions, uncertainty and data limitations and should never be represented as guaranteed reach, conversions, ROAS or revenue.

How does Boost RAVA protect data?

AI features should use privacy-aware, permissioned data practices with access controls and appropriate consent. This page does not claim processing or certifications that have not been independently verified.
