Publisher revenue formula

Filled impressions = Pageviews × ad opportunities/page × eligible rate × fill rate

Gross revenue = Filled impressions ÷ 1,000 × eCPM

Publisher revenue = Gross revenue × publisher revenue share

What is eCPM?

eCPM expresses revenue per 1,000 impressions. For realized data: eCPM = Revenue ÷ Impressions × 1,000.

Fill rate describes the share of eligible ad opportunities that receive an ad. Neither metric is guaranteed.

PUBLISHER QUESTIONS

What publishers ask before monetizing

Direct, factual answers help publishers understand eligibility, monetization, reporting and the controls that matter before activation.

What is Boost RAVA for publishers?

Boost RAVA provides a publisher monetization workspace for managing eligible websites, ad units, reporting, earnings and publisher-side advertising workflows.

How can I become a publisher?

Create a Boost RAVA account, add your website or property, complete the applicable verification or review steps, create supported ad units and install the approved integration method.

How do I add and verify a website?

The publisher workflow is designed around adding properties and managing their status. Exact verification requirements depend on the production review process for the account and property.

How do I create an ad unit?

Create an ad unit for an approved property, choose the supported size or format, configure the placement and use the generated integration method where available.

What ad formats can publishers monetize?

The publisher ecosystem supports common display and mobile sizes plus native, HTML5/rich media and video inventory where the configured placement and demand path support the format.

How does publisher revenue work?

Revenue depends on eligible impressions, advertiser demand, auction or pricing conditions, traffic quality and the account’s monetization configuration. Boost RAVA does not guarantee fill rate, eCPM or earnings.

What is eCPM?

eCPM is an effective revenue metric that expresses estimated or realized earnings per one thousand impressions. It is useful for comparing inventory performance, but it is not a guaranteed payout rate.

How do I estimate publisher ad revenue?

A planning estimate can multiply filled impressions by eCPM and divide by 1,000, then apply the publisher revenue share if the commercial model uses one. Actual earnings depend on valid measured delivery and demand.

How do I calculate fill rate?

Fill rate is the share of eligible ad opportunities that receive an ad. A simple formula is filled impressions divided by eligible ad opportunities, multiplied by 100.

How many impressions do I need for a revenue target?

A reverse estimate can divide the target gross revenue by eCPM and multiply by 1,000. If a revenue share applies, convert the publisher target into the required gross revenue first.

What is header bidding?

Header bidding is a pre-auction technique that can let multiple eligible demand sources compete before a final ad decision. Boost RAVA should only present Prebid/header-bidding support as live after the production integration is verified.

Can I use Google Ad Manager with Boost RAVA?

A GAM integration should only be presented as available after the exact production connection mode is verified. This page keeps that capability clearly status-labeled instead of implying unsupported Google features.

Does Boost RAVA support OpenRTB?

OpenRTB is the standard protocol used for many programmatic bid requests and responses. This page does not claim live OpenRTB exchange connectivity until that production integration is verified.

What happens when there is no suitable advertiser demand?

Fallback or backfill can be used only where configured. The page does not promise guaranteed fill or automatic monetization of every impression.

Can I manage multiple websites?

The publisher product model supports website/property management. The exact account limits and permissions should come from production configuration rather than a marketing claim.

How are invalid or suspicious impressions handled?

Traffic-quality, policy and review controls should be used to protect advertisers and publishers. Any specific invalid-traffic detection capability should be promoted only when the production monitoring path is verified.

When will I receive payment and what is the minimum payout?

Payment timing, eligibility and minimum payout should be shown from the actual publisher payment policy or account configuration. No threshold or schedule is invented on this page.
