The 9 Pillars of Programmatic Infrastructure

A premium infrastructure demands clear distinction: we avoid conflating ad servers, header bidding, OpenRTB, SSPs, and DSPs as synonyms.

LIVE COREDecision & Delivery

1. Ad Serving Core

Central Decisioning Layer

Receives ad requests, validates targeting eligibility, evaluates flight schedules, enforces frequency caps, serves winning creative, and logs billable events.

LIVE COREAuction Dynamics

2. Real-Time Bidding (RTB)

Auction Evaluation Engine

Instantaneous multi-party auction evaluating competing bids within 100 milliseconds under configured floor price and priority rules.

PROTOCOL VERIFIEDStandardized Messaging

3. OpenRTB Protocol

Bid Request / Response Transport

Industry-standardized JSON protocol defining structured bid requests (impressions, device, geo, content) and bid responses (price, creative, ad markup).

INTEGRATION READYHeader Bidding Wrapper

4. Prebid Framework

Client & Server-Side Integration

Open-source header bidding ecosystem allowing publisher pages to solicit bids from multiple programmatic demand partners prior to the primary ad server call.

PRE-AUCTION PATHPublisher Monetization

5. Header Bidding Architecture

Simultaneous Competition

Eliminates traditional waterfall inefficiency by giving external programmatic demand equal opportunity to compete against direct campaigns.

LIVE COREAdvertiser Buying

6. Demand Side (DSP & Direct)

Demand Ingestion

Ingests advertiser campaigns, audience criteria, creative payloads, and budget rules into the programmatic decisioning pipeline.

LIVE COREPublisher Inventory

7. Supply Side (SSP & Inventory)

Ad Opportunity Aggregator

Aggregates publisher properties, ad units, and placement slots into structured inventory with format, device, and floor attributes.

EXCHANGE INTEGRATIONLiquidity Layer

8. Ad Exchange Marketplace

Matching Engine

Neutral programmatic marketplace connecting supply and demand with clear transaction rules, auction clearing, and deduplication.

LIVE COREVerification & Quality

9. Telemetry & Measurement

Audit & Analytics

Logs raw impression requests, valid render events, click verification, latency monitoring, and S2S postback conversion attribution.

KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked Questions on AdTech Infrastructure

Clear technical definitions for ad engineers, media planners, and programmatic buyers.

What is the difference between an ad server and an SSP?+

An ad server is the primary management and delivery engine that decides which ad serves on a publisher’s website based on priority, direct campaign orders, and inventory rules. An SSP (Supply-Side Platform) is a monetization layer designed specifically to connect that inventory to external programmatic demand, DSPs, and ad exchanges.

What is OpenRTB and how does Boost RAVA utilize it?+

OpenRTB is an IAB-maintained protocol that standardizes the structure of bid requests and bid responses across programmatic participants. It ensures that device signals, floor prices, creative attributes, and auction rules are transmitted reliably between demand and supply in milliseconds.

What is the role of Prebid in programmatic infrastructure?+

Prebid is an open-source framework used by publishers to run header bidding auctions directly on the browser (Prebid.js) or via server-to-server wrappers (Prebid Server), maximizing bid density before the ad server makes its final decision.

How does header bidding differ from traditional waterfall setups?+

In a traditional waterfall, ad calls cascade sequentially from one ad network to the next based on historical average rates, resulting in latency and lost revenue. Header bidding allows multiple demand sources to bid simultaneously on the exact same impression, driving true market competition.

How does Boost RAVA handle latency and auction timeouts?+

Boost RAVA applies strict timeout thresholds (typically 80ms–150ms). If a demand partner does not respond within the allocated window, the auction closes gracefully, ensuring that publisher page load speeds and Core Web Vitals remain completely unimpacted.
