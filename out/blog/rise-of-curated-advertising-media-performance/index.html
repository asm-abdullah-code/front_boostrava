The Rise of Curated Advertising: A Smarter Path to Media Performance

Why Curated Marketplaces are Expanding Modern advertisers demand direct relationships with premium publishers while preserving the automation and efficiency of programmatic buying. Curated…

M
Michael Chen
VP of Programmatic Engineering • September 05, 2026
Share:in𝕏
💡 Quick Answer (Summary)

Curated advertising packages curated supply, first-party data assets, and custom targeting rules into programmatic Deal IDs (PMPs). This allows media buyers to access verified high-performing inventory without navigating fragmented open web exchanges.

📌 Key Takeaways

  • ✓Curated marketplaces simplify media planning by bundling premium publisher inventory into turnkey Deal IDs.
  • ✓Supply path optimization (SPO) transparency reduces middleman tech tax by up to 20%.
  • ✓Direct publisher deals ensure 100% brand safety and verified human viewability.

Why Curated Marketplaces are Expanding

Modern advertisers demand direct relationships with premium publishers while preserving the automation and efficiency of programmatic buying. Curated marketplaces bridge this gap through bespoke Deal IDs and transparent supply paths.
