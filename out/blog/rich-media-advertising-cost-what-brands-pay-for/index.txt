15:T824e,<p class="isSelectedEnd">A static banner has a relatively simple job: display a message and invite a click. A rich media advertisement can do considerably more.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">It may expand, animate, play video, respond to scrolling, rotate products, reveal information after interaction, use multiple creative states, or create a high-impact experience around page content. That additional capability is precisely why <strong>rich media advertising cost</strong> cannot be <a href="https://www.boostrava.com/blog/dsp-ssp-rtb-bangladesh/">programmatic ads</a> in the same way as the price of a standard display banner.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">For brands in Bangladesh, the cost of a rich media campaign usually comes from several separate components:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>The price of the advertising inventory</li>
<li>The complexity of the creative</li>
<li>HTML5 or interactive development</li>
<li>Video and design production</li>
<li>Premium placement costs</li>
<li>Audience and geographic targeting</li>
<li>Campaign duration</li>
<li>Measurement and reporting requirements</li>
<li>Frequency and reach</li>
<li>Testing and creative adaptation</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">That means two brands can buy the same number of impressions yet spend very different amounts.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">One may use a lightweight interactive unit across broad inventory. Another may purchase a high-impact takeover on premium content, add video, commission custom HTML5 development, and restrict delivery to a narrow audience.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Both campaigns are &ldquo;rich media,&rdquo; but their economics are completely different.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">This guide explains <strong>what brands actually pay for when buying rich media advertising</strong>, how pricing is commonly structured in Bangladesh, which costs are easy to overlook, and how to decide whether the extra investment is justified.</p>
<h2>Rich Media Advertising Cost in Bangladesh: The Short Answer</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">There is no single market-wide rich media ad price.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Publicly available Bangladesh publisher rate cards reviewed for this guide show how widely the pricing can vary by format. Examples include interactive or parallax inventory offered around <strong>BDT 70&ndash;200 CPM</strong>, richer display or video inventory around <strong>BDT 250&ndash;300 CPM</strong>, and high-impact formats sold on an hourly basis at approximately <strong>BDT 10,000&ndash;30,000 per hour</strong> depending on the placement and implementation. Some public rate cards also list separate rich-media creative production charges around <strong>BDT 50,000</strong>.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">These figures should be treated as <strong>market examples, not a universal rate card</strong>.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Your actual cost may be lower or higher depending on:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Inventory quality</li>
<li>Publisher environment</li>
<li>Ad format</li>
<li>Campaign scale</li>
<li>Audience</li>
<li>Creative requirements</li>
<li>Negotiated commercial terms</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">For brands, the important lesson is that rich media pricing has at least two major layers:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>media cost + creative/technology cost</strong></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A campaign that appears affordable on a CPM basis may become expensive after production requirements are added.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">The reverse can also happen: a relatively expensive creative may be reused across enough impressions that its effective cost per thousand becomes small.</p>
<h2>What Qualifies as Rich Media Advertising?</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Rich media is interactive digital advertising rather than a simple static or basic animated creative.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">IAB defines rich media in measurement guidance as advertising with which users can interact, distinguishing it from an ad that is merely animated.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Depending on the inventory and device, rich media experiences can include:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Expandable banners</li>
<li>Parallax units</li>
<li>Scroll-triggered advertisements</li>
<li>Interactive product cards</li>
<li>3D or rotating creative</li>
<li>In-banner video</li>
<li>Floating units</li>
<li>Full-page experiences</li>
<li>Page takeovers</li>
<li>Swipeable product galleries</li>
<li>Interactive calls to action</li>
<li>HTML5 mini-experiences</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">HTML5 became particularly important for modern rich media <a href="https://www.boostrava.com/blog/programmatic-advertising-cost-bangladesh/">programmatic ads</a> interactive elements can be built for multiple screen environments without relying on obsolete technologies. IAB guidance also stresses efficient loading, deferred heavy assets, compressed files, and optimized delivery to protect the user experience.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">For a Bangladesh advertiser, that technical point has a direct financial implication.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A rich media ad is not merely a larger image.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">It is closer to a <strong>small interactive digital product</strong>, and development effort becomes part of the cost.</p>
<h2>The Five Costs Behind a Rich Media Campaign</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A useful way to understand rich media pricing is to stop asking, &ldquo;What is the CPM?&rdquo; and separate the campaign into five cost buckets.</p>
<table>
<tbody>
<tr>
<th>Cost Component</th>
<th>What the Brand Pays For</th>
<th>Common Pricing Method</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Media</td>
<td>Access to impressions or premium placement</td>
<td>CPM, fixed fee or hourly rate</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Creative</td>
<td>Design, animation, video and copy</td>
<td>One-time production fee</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Development</td>
<td>HTML5 coding and interactive functionality</td>
<td>Project or format fee</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Adaptation</td>
<td>Resizing and device-specific versions</td>
<td>Per variation or package</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Measurement</td>
<td>Interaction tracking, reporting and QA</td>
<td>Included or additional</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Not every campaign contains all five costs.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A simple parallax unit may require only creative adaptation and media.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A custom interactive campaign could involve all five.</p>
<h2>1. Media Cost: The Price of the Inventory</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">The largest recurring expense is usually the cost of delivering the advertisement.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Rich media inventory can be sold through several commercial models.</p>
<h3>CPM Pricing</h3>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">CPM means cost per 1,000 impressions.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">The formula is:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>Media Cost = Impressions &divide; 1,000 &times; CPM</strong></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">If a campaign buys 500,000 rich media impressions at BDT 180 CPM:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>500,000 &divide; 1,000 &times; 180 = BDT 90,000</strong></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">That is the media spend before creative production or other charges.</p>
<h3>Fixed-Time Pricing</h3>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Some high-impact formats are sold by time rather than impression volume.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">For example:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Four-hour takeover</li>
<li>Full-day sponsorship</li>
<li>Homepage roadblock</li>
<li>Hourly floating format</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Public Bangladesh rate cards show that some high-impact rich media inventory is priced hourly, with examples ranging from approximately BDT 10,000 to BDT 30,000 per hour and minimum booking periods applying in some cases.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><img src="https://api.boostrava.com/assets/images/blog/content/2026/08/c8b2c715-f86a-4339-8851-d3cd9574e1e6.webp" alt="" width="1920" height="1080"></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Time-based pricing can be attractive when a brand wants concentrated visibility around a specific moment rather than continuous delivery over several weeks.</p>
<h3>Negotiated Campaign Packages</h3>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Large campaigns may also combine:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Rich media impressions</li>
<li>Premium placement</li>
<li>Multiple creative formats</li>
<li>Homepage exposure</li>
<li>Mobile inventory</li>
<li>Campaign reporting</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">into one negotiated package.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">In that situation, comparing the package with a simple CPM can be misleading because the advertiser is purchasing more than standard impressions.</p>
<h2>2. Creative Production Cost</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">The second major cost is producing the advertisement itself.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A standard static banner might require:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Artwork</li>
<li>Copy</li>
<li>Resizing</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A rich media unit may require:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Storyboarding</li>
<li>Motion design</li>
<li>HTML5 development</li>
<li>Interaction design</li>
<li>Video editing</li>
<li>Coding</li>
<li>Testing</li>
<li>Device adaptation</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">That difference explains why creative fees can become significant.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Public Bangladesh rate-card examples include <strong>BDT 50,000 creative-making charges</strong> for selected HTML5 rich media formats.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Again, this is not a fixed market price.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A simple reusable template may cost considerably less to adapt, while a highly customized brand experience may require a much larger production budget.</p>
<h3>What Increases Creative Cost?</h3>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Creative cost tends to rise when a brand requests:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Multiple interactive states</li>
<li>Custom animations</li>
<li>Embedded video</li>
<li>Product feeds</li>
<li>Multiple CTAs</li>
<li>Advanced transitions</li>
<li>Custom JavaScript</li>
<li>Several screen sizes</li>
<li>Bangla and English versions</li>
<li>Multiple product variations</li>
<li>Complex tracking</li>
<li>Accelerated production deadlines</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">The correct production question is therefore not:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>&ldquo;How much does an HTML5 banner cost?&rdquo;</strong></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">It is:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>&ldquo;How many creative states, interactions, assets and versions are required?&rdquo;</strong></p>
<h2>3. Development and Technical QA</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Rich media must work correctly.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">That sounds obvious, but an interactive advertisement may behave differently across:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Mobile browsers</li>
<li>Desktop browsers</li>
<li>Screen sizes</li>
<li>Publisher environments</li>
<li>Connection speeds</li>

<h2>Why Rich Media CPM Is Usually Higher</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Brands sometimes ask why an interactive advertisement should cost more than a regular display impression.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">There are several reasons.</p>
<h3>Scarcer Inventory</h3>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Not every placement can support advanced interaction.</p>
<h3>Larger Visual Impact</h3>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">High-impact formats may occupy more valuable page real estate.</p>
<h3>Technical Requirements</h3>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Interactive creative may require compatible ad-serving environments.</p>
<h3>Greater Production Effort</h3>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">The creative itself costs more to build and test.</p>
<h3>Premium Publisher Placement</h3>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Rich formats are often offered in locations designed to maximize visibility or attention.</p>
<h3>Operational Complexity</h3>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Creative validation, troubleshooting and reporting may require additional support.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">The premium is therefore not payment for &ldquo;animation.&rdquo;</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">It is payment for a different advertising experience and the infrastructure required to deliver it.</p>
