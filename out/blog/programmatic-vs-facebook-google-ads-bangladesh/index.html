What Programmatic Advertising Vs Facebook Ads Bangladesh Really Means

At its core, programmatic advertising uses software and rules to buy or sell digital advertising opportunities. An advertiser defines an outcome, budget, audience or context, geography, schedule, frequency, creative, and bid logic. A publisher makes eligible inventory available under its own rules. Platforms evaluate whether the opportunity fits, transact through an auction or deal, serve the winning creative, and record delivery and outcome signals.
