15:T8cd2,<p class="isSelectedEnd">Banner advertising remains one of the most recognizable forms of digital advertising in Bangladesh.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">It is also one of the easiest formats to misunderstand.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A business may hear that banner advertising costs BDT 50 CPM, BDT 150 CPM, BDT 300 CPM, or even several thousand taka for a fixed placement and assume one of those figures must be the &ldquo;real&rdquo; market price.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">In practice, <strong>banner advertising cost in Bangladesh and <a href="https://www.boostrava.com/blog/programmatic-advertising-cost-bangladesh/">programmatic ads</a></strong>&nbsp;varies because advertisers are not always buying the same thing.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A standard mobile banner on broad inventory is very different from a premium homepage placement.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A run-of-network campaign is different from targeting finance readers in Dhaka.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A basic image banner is different from an animated HTML5 unit.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A campaign purchased through programmatic bidding is different from a direct fixed-duration booking.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">That means the right question is not simply:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>&ldquo;How much does banner advertising cost in Bangladesh?&rdquo;</strong></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A more useful question is:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>&ldquo;What format, inventory, audience, placement, buying model, and campaign outcome am I paying for?&rdquo;</strong></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">For Bangladesh advertisers, that distinction matters because digital media budgets are increasingly expected to deliver measurable business value&mdash;not only impressions.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">This guide breaks down the main banner formats, pricing structures, budget calculations, cost drivers, and value considerations brands should understand before buying display inventory in Bangladesh.</p>
<h2>Banner Advertising Cost in Bangladesh: The Practical Answer</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">There is no single nationwide banner advertising rate.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Pricing can vary significantly according to:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Publisher quality</li>
<li>Ad position</li>
<li>Banner size</li>
<li>Mobile or desktop inventory</li>
<li>Campaign volume</li>
<li>Geographic targeting</li>
<li>Audience targeting</li>
<li>Content category</li>
<li>Seasonality</li>
<li>Buying method</li>
<li>Creative type</li>
<li>Negotiated commercial terms</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Public Bangladesh publisher rate cards show that banner inventory can be offered through several different pricing models, including CPM, fixed daily or monthly fees, and premium placement packages.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Because those rate cards represent specific publishers rather than the entire market, they should be treated as <strong>reference points&mdash;not universal prices</strong>.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">For planning purposes, brands should separate banner advertising cost into three broad categories:</p>
<h3>Standard Display Inventory</h3>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Usually the most scalable and flexible option.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Often purchased on a CPM basis.</p>
<h3>Premium Display Inventory</h3>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Higher-value positions, stronger publisher environments, or more prominent placements.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Usually costs more than standard inventory.</p>
<h3>Fixed or Sponsored Placements</h3>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Homepage, section sponsorship, masthead, or reserved placements.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">These may be sold by:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Hour</li>
<li>Day</li>
<li>Week</li>
<li>Month</li>
<li>Fixed campaign package</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">The pricing model matters almost as much as the headline price.</p>
<h2>What Is Banner Advertising?</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Banner advertising refers to graphical advertisements displayed within websites, digital content environments, and other compatible online inventory.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A banner may contain:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Brand logo</li>
<li>Product image</li>
<li>Headline</li>
<li>Offer</li>
<li>Call to action</li>
<li>Animation</li>
<li>HTML5 elements</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Banner advertising can support objectives such as:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Brand awareness</li>
<li>Product discovery</li>
<li>Website traffic</li>
<li>Lead generation</li>
<li>Retargeting</li>
<li>E-commerce sales</li>
<li>Seasonal campaigns</li>
<li>Product launches</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">The format itself is relatively simple.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">The strategic value comes from <strong>where the banner appears, who sees it, what it communicates, and what happens after the impression or click</strong>.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><img src="https://api.boostrava.com/assets/images/blog/content/2026/08/999d0c1f-d649-4c7f-bc3f-7ba91e9c3a09.webp" alt="" width="1920" height="1080"></p>

<p class="isSelectedEnd">The following are common planning formats rather than a guarantee that every publisher supports every size.</p>

<p class="isSelectedEnd">IAB Tech Lab's display advertising standards have long helped the industry organize digital ad units around common sizes and flexible creative principles.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">For advertisers, standardization matters because it can reduce production cost and make creative easier to deploy across multiple publishers and programmatic inventory sources.</p>
<h2>Why Banner Size Affects Cost</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">The cost difference between banner sizes is not simply based on pixels.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Larger or more prominent units may cost more because they can offer:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Greater visibility</li>
<li>More page space</li>
<li>Lower supply availability</li>
<li>Higher advertiser demand</li>
<li>Better creative storytelling</li>
<li>Stronger attention potential</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A 300&times;250 unit can exist across a large number of placements.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A large homepage billboard may be available only once or a few times on a page.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Scarcity changes commercial value.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">That is why advertisers should not assume that all banner impressions should have the same CPM.</p>
<h2>How Banner Advertising Is Priced in Bangladesh</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">There are several common ways digital publishers and advertising platforms can price banner campaigns.</p>
<h2>CPM Pricing</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>CPM</strong> means cost per 1,000 impressions.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">It is one of the most common digital advertising pricing methods.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">The formula is:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>Banner Media Cost = Impressions &divide; 1,000 &times; CPM</strong></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Suppose a campaign buys:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>1,000,000 impressions</li>
<li>BDT 100 CPM</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">The media spend is:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>1,000,000 &divide; 1,000 &times; 100 = BDT 100,000</strong></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">CPM is useful because it makes campaigns with different impression volumes easier to compare.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">But CPM alone does not measure business value.</p>
<h2>CPC Pricing</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Some campaigns may be evaluated or purchased around <strong>cost per click</strong>.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">CPC formula:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>CPC = Total Spend &divide; Number of Clicks</strong></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">If an advertiser spends BDT 20,000 and receives 1,000 clicks:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>CPC = BDT 20</strong></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">CPC helps measure traffic efficiency.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">However, a cheap click does not necessarily mean a valuable visitor.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A higher CPC campaign can still produce better returns if the visitors are more likely to buy.</p>
<h2>Fixed-Time Pricing</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Premium banner inventory may be sold for a fixed period.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Examples can include:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Homepage banner for 24 hours</li>
<li>Section takeover for one week</li>
<li>Header banner for one month</li>
<li>Event-day placement</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Fixed pricing can make sense when the advertiser values guaranteed visibility within a specific publisher environment.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Unlike CPM, the number of impressions may vary depending on publisher traffic during the booked period.</p>
<h2>Fixed Placement Packages</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Publishers may also bundle several placements together.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">For example:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Homepage leaderboard</li>
<li>Article rectangle</li>
<li>Mobile banner</li>
<li>Section sponsorship</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A package may look expensive compared with one standard CPM placement but could include inventory that is not directly comparable.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Advertisers should request a breakdown when possible.</p>
<h2>What Actually Determines Banner CPM?</h2>

<h2>4. Geographic Targeting</h2>

<h2>What Does a Banner Campaign Really Cost?</h2>

<h2>A Practical Banner Budget Example</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Imagine a Bangladesh retailer planning a campaign.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Campaign plan:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>2,000,000 impressions</li>
<li>BDT 90 CPM</li>
<li>Four banner sizes</li>
<li>BDT 30,000 creative production cost</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Media cost:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>2,000,000 &divide; 1,000 &times; BDT 90 = BDT 180,000</strong></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Creative cost:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>BDT 30,000</strong></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Total campaign cost:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>BDT 210,000</strong></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">All-in effective CPM:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>BDT 105</strong></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">The advertiser may have purchased media at BDT 90 CPM, but the total economic cost of the campaign is approximately BDT 105 per 1,000 impressions.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">That figure can be more useful when comparing campaigns.</p>
<h2>Why Creative Cost Matters More for Small Campaigns</h2>

<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>BDT 100 per 1,000 impressions</strong></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">If the same creative is used across 3 million impressions, the production cost contributes:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>BDT 10 per 1,000 impressions</strong></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Campaign scale changes the economics.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Small advertisers can often control cost by:</p>

<p class="isSelectedEnd">A BDT 60 CPM campaign may be excellent if it reaches the right people and drives business value.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A BDT 250 CPM campaign may also be excellent if <a href="https://dribbble.com/search/ad-placement">premium ads placement</a> delivers the visibility the brand needs.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">And either campaign can fail if the audience, creative, landing page, or measurement is poor.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">For Bangladesh advertisers, the best way to evaluate banner advertising cost is therefore not to search for one &ldquo;correct&rdquo; market price.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Instead, compare:</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>format + placement + audience + volume + campaign objective + total cost + measurable outcome</strong></p>
