Payment Policy

Rules for advertiser funding, campaign charges, publisher earnings, transaction review, billing records and the Boost RAVA no-refund policy.

Effective: 30 August 2026 · Last updated: 30 August 2026

Boost RAVA LegalPrivacy PolicyTerms & ConditionsPayment PolicyCookie PolicyContact
Important: Boost RAVA follows a no-refund model. Payments and funds accepted or credited to an account are non-refundable except where a refund is strictly required by applicable law. This page intentionally does not promise availability of any specific third-party payment processor.
01

1. Scope

This Payment Policy applies to advertiser account funding, campaign charges, platform or service fees, publisher earnings and payouts, transaction adjustments and related financial records within Boost RAVA. Account-specific commercial agreements may add terms; where they conflict, the signed agreement controls to the extent permitted by law.

02

2. Advertiser funding and campaign spend

Advertisers may be required to maintain sufficient account balance or complete an approved payment step before a campaign can be activated or continue delivery. Available funding methods can vary by account, currency, country, risk review and platform configuration.

Campaign spend can accrue according to the pricing model, eligible delivery and reporting applicable to the campaign. Budgets and estimates are controls or planning inputs, not guarantees that the full amount will be spent or that a particular result will be achieved.

03

3. No-refund policy

All completed payments, deposits, account funding, campaign funding, platform charges and other amounts accepted or credited by Boost RAVA are final and non-refundable, except where applicable law strictly requires otherwise.

No refund is created by pausing, stopping, deleting, expiring or under-delivering a campaign; changing targeting or creative; closing an account; failing to use an available balance; dissatisfaction with performance; differences between third-party analytics and Boost RAVA reporting; inventory becoming unavailable; or a user’s failure to comply with policy.

Before making a payment, users should review the amount, currency, account, campaign and intended use carefully.

04

4. Transaction errors and unauthorized activity

A suspected duplicate processing error, incorrect transaction record or unauthorized account activity should be reported promptly through the Contact page. We may investigate logs, account access, transaction references and related records. Correcting a demonstrable processing error is an account reconciliation action and does not change the general no-refund policy.

Users must not initiate abusive disputes, false charge claims or payment reversals for validly delivered services. We may restrict accounts while a payment dispute, fraud review or negative balance remains unresolved.

05

5. Pricing, currency and taxes

Prices, bid values, budgets, fees, balances and publisher earnings may be displayed or processed in currencies supported by the relevant account configuration. Currency conversion, intermediary, bank, transfer or similar charges may apply outside Boost RAVA and are not necessarily controlled by us.

Users are responsible for taxes, duties, levies, withholding and reporting obligations applicable to their business unless applicable law or a written agreement requires Boost RAVA to collect, deduct or remit them.

06

6. Invoices, records and reconciliation

Boost RAVA may maintain invoices, transaction IDs, balance entries, campaign spend records, payout records and other financial logs. Users should review records promptly and report a suspected discrepancy with the relevant account, date, amount and reference.

Advertising metrics and financial records may be adjusted to reflect invalid traffic, duplicate events, corrections, partner reconciliation, policy enforcement, credits required by law, or other verified accounting adjustments.

07

7. Publisher earnings

Publisher dashboard revenue can be estimated, pending, provisional, confirmed or otherwise subject to reconciliation depending on the workflow. An impression or dashboard entry does not automatically create an unconditional payment obligation.

Earnings may be reviewed for traffic quality, invalid activity, policy compliance, inventory ownership, advertiser settlement, reporting discrepancies and required deductions before becoming payment-eligible.

08

8. Publisher payouts

Eligible publisher payouts are processed according to the payment method, minimum threshold, schedule and verification conditions shown in the publisher account or separately agreed with the publisher. We do not publish a universal payout threshold or guaranteed payment date on this policy unless it is actually configured for the account.

A payout can be delayed, held, adjusted or rejected where information is incomplete, ownership cannot be verified, traffic is invalid or suspicious, the account violates policy, required tax/compliance information is missing, or a legal/security review requires it.

09

9. Invalid traffic, fraud and deductions

Boost RAVA may exclude or deduct revenue associated with invalid impressions, invalid clicks, fabricated conversions, bots, incentivized or manipulated activity, domain or inventory misrepresentation, policy violations, duplicate events or other traffic that does not qualify for payment.

Fraud review can occur before or after preliminary dashboard reporting. Deliberate manipulation may result in suspension or termination in addition to financial adjustment.

10

10. Negative balances and outstanding amounts

If corrections, disputes, invalid activity, fees or other valid adjustments create an amount owed to Boost RAVA, we may offset that amount against available balances or future amounts payable where permitted. Access to paid services may be restricted until an outstanding amount is resolved.

11

11. Campaign cancellation and unused funds

A campaign may be paused or cancelled subject to platform controls, but cancellation does not convert prior payment or account funding into a refundable amount. Any treatment of an unused internal balance is governed by the account configuration, applicable law and any written commercial agreement; it should not be assumed to be cash-withdrawable or refundable.

12

12. Payment security

Users should submit payment or payout information only through authorized Boost RAVA workflows or verified instructions. Never send passwords, secret API keys or full sensitive credentials through ordinary support messages. Boost RAVA may apply transaction, account or identity review to reduce fraud and abuse.

13

13. Changes and contact

We may update payment processes, supported methods, thresholds, schedules or this policy as the platform and regulatory requirements evolve. Changes do not create retroactive refunds for previously completed transactions unless required by law.

Questions about these policies can be submitted through the Boost RAVA Contact page. Please include enough account, campaign, publisher-property, billing, or technical context for us to identify the matter without sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.
