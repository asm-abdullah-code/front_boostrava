The 6 Core Contextual Targeting Signals

Combining environmental, temporal, and semantic indicators to make media decisions precise without relying on personal user data.

Core Signal

1. Content Context

Deep semantic analysis evaluating page keywords, article topics, editorial sentiment, and brand suitability categories.

Real Example: A reader exploring a smartphone review sees mobile accessory and telecom ads.
Geo Precision

2. Location & Geography

Targeting by country, division, and cities (such as Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet) using IP and network signals without tracking GPS history.

Real Example: Deliver hyperlocal restaurant or retail promos strictly to Dhaka metro readers.
Hardware Adaptive

3. Device & Screen Type

Differentiates between mobile touchscreens, tablets, desktop monitors, and connected smart TVs to adapt creative layouts.

Real Example: Serve thumb-friendly mobile rich media to smartphone users and mastheads to desktop.
Bilingual Support

4. Content Language

Accurately distinguishes between English, Bengali (বাংলা), and mixed Banglish content environments for natural cultural alignment.

Real Example: Serve Bengali-copy creatives on Bengali news dailies and English copy on business portals.
Temporal Focus

5. Time & Dayparting

Activates ad delivery during peak reader attention hours, commuting slots, weekend leisure, or specific flight schedules.

Real Example: Promote breakfast snacks from 7 AM–10 AM and dinner deliveries from 6 PM–9 PM.
Privacy Preserved

6. Audience Intent Segments

Groups readers into contextual interest cohorts (e.g. Auto Enthusiasts, Tech Buyers, Home Chefs) based on active reading patterns.

Real Example: Engage serious business decision-makers reading financial and corporate news.
KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked Questions About Contextual Ads

Clear answers on semantic matching, privacy preservation, and brand suitability.

What is contextual advertising?+

Contextual advertising is an automated marketing method that aligns ads with the actual content, topic, and environment of the webpage being viewed, rather than tracking individual users across the internet.

How does contextual advertising differ from behavioral targeting?+

Behavioral targeting relies on cookies, device identifiers, and historical tracking to predict user interests. Contextual advertising matches ads based entirely on the active page environment (topic, location, language, time), ensuring 100% compliance with modern privacy regulations.

How does the visual flow Content → Context → Signal → Relevance → Ad work?+

First, Content is ingested from the publisher page. Second, natural language processing extracts the Context (topic, category, sentiment). Third, non-personal Signals (location, device, language, time) are evaluated. Fourth, the Relevance engine scores matching campaigns. Finally, the optimal Ad creative is served in milliseconds.

Can contextual advertising work with Bengali and local Bangladesh content?+

Yes. Boost RAVA supports contextual classification across Bengali (বাংলা) and English media in Bangladesh, identifying news topics, sports commentary, and lifestyle themes for hyper-relevant campaign placement.

Does contextual targeting support brand safety and suitability?+

Yes. Contextual advertising actively prevents ads from appearing alongside sensitive news, disasters, political controversy, or inappropriate content through negative keyword filters and category exclusions.
