CONTEXTUAL ADVERTISING 101

What Is Contextual Advertising?

Contextual advertising is an approach where surrounding content, topic, category, keywords and other page signals are analyzed to determine relevant advertising opportunities.

EXAMPLEArticle: “How to Choose the Right Tea”Tea → Beverage → Food → LifestylePotential relevant demand: Tea brands · accessories · beverage products
Definition

Context first. Identity not required for the core match.

The broader ad stack may still use other permitted signals, so contextual advertising should be described as privacy-aware—not as a guarantee that no personal data exists anywhere in the system.

QUESTIONS ADVERTISERS & PUBLISHERS ASK

Contextual Advertising, Explained Clearly.

What is contextual advertising?+

Contextual advertising matches advertising opportunities to the content and environment of a page. It focuses on signals such as topic, category, keywords and page meaning rather than relying only on an individual user profile.

How does contextual advertising work?+

A contextual system analyzes a page, identifies contextual signals, maps them to topics or categories and then uses those signals to determine which eligible campaigns are relevant for that environment.

Does contextual advertising use personal data?+

Contextual advertising can work from page and content signals without depending primarily on personal profiles. The wider advertising stack may still use other permitted signals, so privacy and consent requirements still apply.

How does Boost RAVA understand page context?+

Boost RAVA positions contextual intelligence around page topic, category, keywords, language, theme, suitability and placement signals. AI or semantic classification should be treated as live only when the production classifier is verified.

What is semantic contextual targeting?+

Semantic contextual targeting looks beyond exact words to the meaning and relationships in content. For example, “Apple smartphone” and “apple farming” contain the same word but belong to different contexts.

Can I target specific topics?+

Topic and category targeting are part of the contextual campaign design. Exact live taxonomy and controls must match what is available in the advertiser workflow.

Can I exclude topics?+

Contextual campaign design can include negative topics, categories, keywords, publishers or placements where those exclusion controls are available in production.

Can I use contextual targeting with programmatic advertising?+

Yes conceptually: contextual signals can become eligibility or decision inputs before a programmatic auction. The exact RTB/OpenRTB wiring should be described only as implemented.

Can contextual advertising work with HTML5 ads?+

Yes. Context determines where an eligible campaign may be relevant, while HTML5 describes the creative technology used to build and deliver the ad.

Can contextual advertising work with Rich Media?+

Yes. Contextual relevance can help determine where a campaign is eligible, while Rich Media defines the richer interactive advertising experience.

Can publishers monetize contextual inventory?+

Context classification can help publishers understand inventory by topic or content category and connect relevant demand where the monetization workflow supports it.

Can contextual advertising work with Bengali content?+

Bengali and mixed-language contextual classification should be labelled supported only after the production language pipeline is verified. Bangladesh-focused contextual use cases can still be planned without claiming unverified language-model support.

Can I combine contextual targeting with other campaign targeting?+

Contextual signals can be combined with supported campaign controls such as geography, device or schedule when those controls are available in the advertiser workflow.

How is contextual advertising performance measured?+

Campaign reporting can measure impressions, clicks, CTR, spend and conversions. Context-level breakdowns such as topic, category or keyword require the reporting pipeline to persist those dimensions.
