1. Introduction

By using our platform, you agree to this Policy.

This Payment Policy governs all financial transactions, payment methods, billing practices, and payout procedures associated with the Boostrava Ads Network.

2. Definitions

  • Client — an advertiser, agency, or other buyer using the DSP.
  • Publisher — a website, app, or CTV channel owner using the SSP.
  • Service Order — any agreement specifying campaign terms.
  • Net Revenue — gross revenue from ad sales after deduction of third-party fees such as ad exchange fees, data costs, and VAT/GST where applicable.

3. Fees for Clients (Advertisers)

Boostrava offers the following fee models, as selected in the Service Order or campaign settings.

CPM (Cost Per Mille) — cost per 1,000 impressions.
CPC (Cost Per Click) — cost per valid click.
CPA (Cost Per Action) — cost per specified action such as purchase or sign-up.
Fixed CPD (Cost Per Day) — flat daily rate for reserved inventory.

Invoicing & Payment Terms

  • Invoices are issued monthly or as agreed in the Service Order.
  • Payment is due within thirty (30) calendar days of the invoice date, unless otherwise specified.
  • Payments must be made in USD, EUR, GBP, or BDT as specified on the invoice.
  • Accepted methods: bank wire transfer, credit card via Stripe, PayPal, or other available methods.

Late Payments & Taxes

  • Late payments incur interest at 1.5% per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law, calculated daily.
  • Boostrava may suspend or terminate access to the DSP for any overdue invoice exceeding fifteen (15) days.
  • The Client is responsible for all taxes, duties, and levies imposed by any authority on the fees, except taxes on Boostrava’s net income.
  • If withholding tax applies, the Client must provide valid tax receipts or certificates.

4. Payments to Publishers

Publisher earns a percentage of Net Revenue generated from ad inventory sold via Boostrava’s SSP.
Payments are made on a net-30 or net-60 basis, as selected in the Publisher dashboard.
Minimum payout threshold is $50.00 USD or equivalent in other currencies.
Payment methods include bank transfer, PayPal, Payoneer, or other methods made available.
Refunds or chargebacks may be deducted from future earnings for invalid traffic, advertiser wins, or technical overpayment errors.

Payment Schedule & Threshold

  • Payments are made on a net-30 or net-60 basis, selected in the Publisher dashboard.
  • The minimum payout threshold is $50.00 USD, or equivalent in other currencies.
  • Unpaid balances below the threshold will roll over to the next payment cycle.

Currency & Conversion

  • Payments are made in the currency selected in the Publisher dashboard.
  • If conversion is required, Boostrava uses the mid-market rate at the time of payment.
  • Publishers are responsible for ensuring correct payment details.
  • Boostrava is not liable for payments sent to incorrect details provided by the Publisher.

5. General Payment Provisions

Neither party may withhold or set off any amount due without prior written consent.
Boostrava may audit Client or Publisher usage and traffic to verify compliance.
Boostrava may suspend all services until outstanding amounts are paid.
Payment disputes must be raised in writing within thirty (30) days of the invoice or payment statement date.
Payment records are retained for seven (7) years for tax and legal compliance.

Chargebacks & Refunds

Boostrava may deduct from future Publisher earnings any amounts refunded to advertisers or charged back due to invalid traffic, fraudulent impressions or clicks generated by the Publisher, advertiser disputes resolved in the advertiser’s favour, or force majeure / technical errors that result in overpayment.

6. Amendments

Boostrava may update this Payment Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified via email or dashboard notice at least fifteen (15) days in advance. Continued use of the platform after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
