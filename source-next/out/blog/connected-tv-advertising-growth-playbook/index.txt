Examples explain strategy and technology; they are not performance guarantees.</p><section class="space-y-4"><h2 class="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold tracking-tight text-foreground leading-tight">Why CTV belongs in a programmatic media plan</h2><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">Connected TV brings internet-delivered television inventory into an environment where advertisers can apply programmatic planning and measurement. It combines the attention of television-style viewing with digital controls such as audience selection, pacing and reporting. That does not make CTV identical to web video; the screen, viewing context and inventory relationships are different.</p><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">For advertisers, the opportunity is to reach viewers on large screens while coordinating CTV with display, video, rich media or other channels. The challenge is to manage frequency and measurement so the campaign is genuinely incremental rather than simply duplicating exposure.</p></section><section class="space-y-4"><h2 class="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold tracking-tight text-foreground leading-tight">Inventory quality and supply path matter</h2><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">CTV inventory can originate from apps, streaming services, device environments and programmatic marketplaces. Advertisers should understand what they are buying, whether the app or channel is identified, how the supply path is represented and what controls exist for unsuitable content or invalid traffic.</p><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">Private marketplace or curated supply arrangements can be useful when an advertiser needs more control. Open-auction access can add scale, but transparency should not be sacrificed for reach.</p></section><section class="space-y-4"><h2 class="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold tracking-tight text-foreground leading-tight">CTV creative should be designed for the television screen</h2><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">A television viewing experience is usually lean-back and sound-enabled. Creative should communicate quickly, use readable text and respect the technical requirements of the placement. Repurposing a small mobile asset without adaptation can reduce clarity on a large screen.</p><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">Interactive behaviour also differs. Some CTV environments support limited interaction while others are primarily video experiences. Campaign teams should design the creative and call to action around what the actual device and app support.</p></section><section class="space-y-4"><h2 class="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold tracking-tight text-foreground leading-tight">Audience and contextual strategy</h2><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">CTV targeting can use permitted audience signals, content context, geography, device information and deal-specific inventory criteria. Contextual signals can be particularly valuable when the surrounding programme or genre is relevant to the brand message.</p><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">Advertisers should avoid over-narrowing. Television-style reach can lose value if the campaign is constrained to tiny segments that cannot accumulate enough delivery or learning.</p></section><section class="space-y-4"><h2 class="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold tracking-tight text-foreground leading-tight">Measurement beyond completed views</h2><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">Video completion rate is useful, but it is not a complete business outcome. CTV measurement may include reach, frequency, attention proxies, site visits, brand studies or conversion signals where technically and legally appropriate. The campaign objective should determine which metrics matter.</p><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">Cross-device attribution can be imperfect, so teams should document methodology and avoid presenting modelled outcomes as directly observed facts. Transparent measurement is more valuable than an impressive but unexplained number.</p></section><section class="space-y-4"><h2 class="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold tracking-tight text-foreground leading-tight">Frequency and cross-screen coordination</h2><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">A viewer may encounter the same campaign on CTV, mobile web, apps and desktop. Without cross-screen planning, frequency can become excessive. Programmatic controls should aim to coordinate exposure where signals permit, while accepting that identity and device matching are not complete in every environment.</p><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">Creative sequencing can also help. CTV can introduce a brand story while display or rich media provides a later interactive step. The channels should have distinct roles instead of repeating the same asset everywhere.</p></section><section class="space-y-4"><h2 class="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold tracking-tight text-foreground leading-tight">CTV opportunities in Bangladesh and international campaigns</h2><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">Bangladesh is a mobile-first digital market, but connected viewing continues to expand as streaming behaviour and smart-device adoption grow. Advertisers should plan CTV based on actual available inventory and audience behaviour rather than assuming television patterns from another country.</p><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">International campaigns need market-specific supply, language, regulation and measurement choices. A cross-market CTV plan should separate global brand consistency from local media execution.</p></section><section class="space-y-4"><h2 class="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold tracking-tight text-foreground leading-tight">Frequently asked questions</h2><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">What is connected TV advertising? It is advertising delivered through internet-connected television environments such as smart TV apps and streaming devices.</p><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">Is CTV programmatic? CTV inventory can be bought programmatically, but not every CTV transaction uses an open auction.</p><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">Can CTV work with AI programmatic optimization? AI can assist with pacing, valuation, contextual classification and other decisions where data and platform capabilities support it.</p><p class="text-muted-foreground leading-8">Is CTV the same as online video? No. Both use video, but screen context, inventory, interaction and measurement differ.</p></section><section class="space-y-4 rounded-2xl border border-border p-6"><h2 class="text-2xl
