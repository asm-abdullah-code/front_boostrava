WHAT IS CTV?

Television Has Become Connected. Advertising Has Too.

Connected TV refers to television viewing through internet-connected TVs and devices, enabling digital-style advertising capabilities around streaming and TV content.

TRADITIONAL TV

Broad audience delivery

Linear or broadcast-style media with more limited digital-style campaign controls and measurement.

CONNECTED TV

Digital delivery on the TV screen

Internet-connected environments with measurable campaign signals and flexible controls where the supply path supports them.

CTV IN ONE SENTENCE

TV viewing delivered through connected devices and streaming environments.

Boost RAVA provides access through partner-powered supply rather than claiming owned CTV inventory.

COMPLETED VIEWS

QUESTIONS ADVERTISERS ASK

CTV Advertising, Explained Clearly.

Short, factual answers designed for humans first—and structured clearly enough for search and answer engines.

What is Connected TV advertising?+

Connected TV advertising delivers ads within internet-connected television and streaming environments. It combines television-style viewing with digital campaign delivery and measurement where the inventory partner supports those capabilities.

What is the difference between CTV and traditional TV?+

Traditional TV is generally bought and measured around broadcast or linear audience delivery. CTV is delivered through connected devices and streaming environments, allowing digital-style campaign controls and measurement signals where supported.

Can I run CTV campaigns in Europe?+

Yes, Boost RAVA can help advertisers explore partner-powered CTV opportunities in selected European markets. Availability varies by supply partner, inventory, market and campaign requirements.

Can I run CTV campaigns in the Middle East?+

Yes, selected Middle East markets can be explored through third-party CTV and programmatic supply partners. Country-level availability is confirmed during planning.

Can a Bangladesh-based business advertise on CTV?+

Yes. A Bangladesh-based advertiser can use Boost RAVA to explore international CTV inventory where partner supply is available and the campaign meets inventory requirements.

Does Boost RAVA own CTV inventory?+

No claim is made that Boost RAVA owns the CTV inventory shown on this page. CTV access is positioned as partner-powered supply from third-party CTV, media and programmatic inventory sources.

How does Boost RAVA access CTV inventory?+

Boost RAVA connects campaign planning and activation to eligible third-party CTV, media or programmatic supply partners. Exact supply depends on the partner and campaign.

Is CTV advertising programmatic?+

CTV inventory can be bought programmatically when the relevant supply path supports automated buying and auction-based delivery. Not every CTV placement is necessarily bought the same way.

What CTV ad formats are available?+

Common partner-supported CTV opportunities can include pre-roll, mid-roll, post-roll, in-stream video and CTV video placements. Availability varies by inventory partner and environment.

What video specifications are required?+

Video duration, aspect ratio, resolution, file format, bitrate, audio and safe-area requirements depend on the inventory partner. Exact specifications should be confirmed before launch.

Can I target specific countries?+

Country or market targeting may be available depending on partner inventory. A listed market is an opportunity to explore, not a guarantee that inventory is available for every campaign.

Can I control frequency?+

Frequency controls may be available depending on the partner, identity method and campaign setup. Device- or household-level limits should only be treated as available when the partner supports them.

How is CTV performance measured?+

CTV reporting can include impressions, spend, CPM, video views, completion and other partner-provided dimensions. Reach, frequency, viewability and conversion reporting depend on available measurement data.

Can I measure completed video views?+

Completed-view and completion-rate reporting can be used when the supply partner returns reliable video-event data for the selected inventory.

How much does CTV advertising cost?+

CTV pricing varies by market, inventory, content environment, targeting, format and supply partner. The calculator on this page is an arithmetic planning tool, not a price quote or inventory guarantee.

Can I combine CTV with display advertising?+

Yes. CTV can support awareness and video storytelling while display can extend digital reach and interaction across other screens.

Can I combine CTV with contextual advertising?+

Yes where the selected inventory supports relevant contextual or content signals. Contextual targeting and CTV supply remain separate capabilities and should only be combined when supported.

Is CTV inventory guaranteed in every country?+

No. Availability depends on partner supply, market, inventory and campaign requirements.
